About Aeduplay

A quarter century of building joyful classrooms

Navshilp Enterprises began in 1999 with a simple promise: make school infrastructure that educators can rely on. Today, the Aeduplay brand equips over a thousand institutions across South India.

Our story

From a boards workshop in George Town to South India's learning-space partner

Navshilp Enterprises was founded in 1999 in George Town, Chennai, as a specialist manufacturer of school writing boards. Word travelled the way it does among educators — quietly, and through results. Boards that didn't warp. Deliveries that arrived on time. A phone that got answered.

As our school partners grew, so did their needs — and so did we. Classroom furniture followed, then laboratory and library fittings. In 2010 we launched Aeduplay, a brand dedicated to play-based learning: outdoor play systems, soft-play zones, preschool furniture and safety flooring.

Today we are an ISO-certified manufacturer serving schools, preschools, colleges, IT parks, hospitals and townships across Tamil Nadu and South India — still family-run, still in Chennai, and still answering the phone.
